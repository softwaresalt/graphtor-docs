## F1 — Selection-gate verdict: NOT PROVEN

The adversarial pre-PR gate (majority P1) held that `056.026-T` may move from
`selection:pending` to `selection:selected` **only if exact-CLI evidence proves a
`type`/`transport` mismatch**, and that archived `056.019-T` plus the H3-A evidence are
insufficient for that specific claim.

An exhaustive search of all durable artifacts (docs/decisions, docs/closure, docs/compound,
docs/memory, exec-plans, `.backlogit/archive`, source, and probe logs) found **NO exact-CLI
evidence of a `type`/`transport` functional mismatch.** The authoritative evidence:

1. **`docs/decisions/2026-08-29-mcp-serve-discover-preinitialize-evidence.md` (E1 + causal
   conclusion).** E1 proves only that a graphtor server **process reached STDIO startup**
   (preflight completed, the embedding model loaded, and `starting MCP STDIO server` was
   logged) **before rmcp rejected a pre-`initialize` `server/discover` request** and the
   process exited `2`. The evidenced regression cause is **H3-A** — server-side rmcp 1.5
   rejecting that pre-`initialize` `server/discover` — i.e. a server-side handshake failure
   rather than a client config-parse failure. Critically, **E1 does NOT record the launching
   configuration or the discriminator field it carried**, so E1 does **not** prove whether
   `type` or `transport` was the honored discriminator, and it is **not** affirmative evidence
   for or against a launch/parse-blocking discriminator mismatch. The current `.mcp.json`
   shape is likewise **not** historical proof of what discriminator was honored at the time of
   the regression.
2. **`.backlogit/archive/056.019-T.md` (H3-B terminal, archived/`done`).** Its acceptance
   criteria state verbatim: "H3-B1 does NOT by itself select the `type`/`transport`
   discriminator tasks (056.026 generation / 056.027 existing-install delivery); those are
   selected ONLY when exact-CLI evidence independently shows a `type`/`transport` mismatch
   (else not-needed)." 056.019-T therefore explicitly **disclaims** being the selector.
3. **Plan `T2g` (056.026-T).** Selection is conditional on **T0 actual-client proof**. The plan
   repeatedly refuses to treat the static docs/generator field-shape difference (siblings use
   `type: "stdio"`; `managed_server_value` emits `transport: "stdio"`) as proof: "it does not
   prove Copilot honors the field" and "no one claims the field name is the current root cause
   without T0 evidence." The static difference is a docs/generator inconsistency, not an
   exact-CLI-honored-field mismatch.

**Verdict: NOT PROVEN.** No evidence was invented, and no controlled exact-CLI evidence
establishing a **functional** `type`/`transport` mismatch was found. Per `056.026-T`'s own
acceptance criterion, the disposition is **`done` + `not-needed: no type/transport mismatch
evidenced`**, evidence passed through unchanged. `selection:pending` → `selection:not-needed`.
