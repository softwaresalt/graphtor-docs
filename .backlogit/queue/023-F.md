Optimize the current pdf-extract-based ingestion path to handle large PDFs (100+ MB) within acceptable time bounds. Grounded in spike findings from docs/decisions/2026-05-03-large-pdf-ingestion-strategy-spike.md. Five ordered optimization steps: (1) eliminate fallback reparse, (2) large-file heuristic for page-oriented mode, (3) suppress repeated glyph warnings, (4) histogram sampling from first N pages, (5) PdfBackend trait boundary for future library swaps.
