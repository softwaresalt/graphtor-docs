Recent GitHub Copilot CLI builds fail to connect to graphtor-docs because rmcp 1.5 terminates when the client's first non-ping pre-initialize request is `server/discover` (H3-A, selected). Restore connectivity evidence-first and test-first with a one-method transport compatibility response/continuation, normal initialize, and no arbitrary pre-initialize dispatch. The selected remedy remains after the frozen `049-S` evidence shipment, PHASE 1.5 probe CI, and the dependency-closed PHASE 1.6 `053-S` unit. Cargo 1.75 cannot parse the current edition-2024 rmcp 1.5.0 manifest; `056.029-T` resolves that dependency graph at the unchanged Rust 2021 / rust-version 1.75 floor, while `056.030-T` through `056.033-T` align CI and declarations. Full diagnosis: `docs/decisions/2026-08-21-mcp-serve-initialize-os-error-232-deliberation.md`; H3-A evidence: `docs/decisions/2026-08-29-mcp-serve-discover-preinitialize-evidence.md`; plan: `docs/exec-plans/2026-08-21-mcp-serve-initialize-handshake-regression-plan.md`.
