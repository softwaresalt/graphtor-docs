SELECTED 2026-08-29 (`selection:selected`). Proven defect: an unhandled pre-`initialize` `server/discover` request is fatal. Live actual-client evidence identifies it as the first non-ping request (`id: 0`); rmcp 1.5 admits only ping in its pre-initialize loop, returns `ServerInitializeError::ExpectedInitializeRequest`, `cmd_serve` exits 2, and the client pipe closes. The remedy is only a private binary-owned transport interposer between `rmcp::transport::stdio()` and `rmcp::serve_server` that handles that one pre-initialize method, keeps the connection alive, then requires normal `initialize` and normal tools. It is the sole member of 052-S after 049-S and PHASE 1.5 (`056.028-T`). `056.011-T` owns the single actual declared-MS​​RV entry check; a nonzero result blocks and returns 052-S to Stage for `056.029-T`, rather than allowing an adapter or compatibility claim to continue. Exact-Copilot before/after evidence, not the draft alone, must prove the response shape; T4 (`056.004-T`) alone owns restored-production acceptance.
