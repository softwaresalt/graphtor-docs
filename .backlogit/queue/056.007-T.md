CONDITIONAL on T0/T1 evidencing lock contention / stale-lock PID REUSE (H0b) rather than the launch-cwd discovery cause (H0a). Isolated out of T2 (056.003-T) so that task implements exactly one evidenced cause. Harden stale-lock liveness in src/lock.rs by recording process start-time alongside pid so a reused pid is not misread as a live lock holder. A matching pid+start-time identity stays LIVE regardless of lock age; the STALE_SECS age check evicts ONLY as a fallback when strong identity is unavailable (legacy pid-only lock), with legacy compatibility and concurrent-release NotFound behavior preserved. Age alone MUST NOT evict a verified live holder. Prefer start-time+pid over a --force escape hatch. Do NOT weaken exclusion of a genuinely live holder.
