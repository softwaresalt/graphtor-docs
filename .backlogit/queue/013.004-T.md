File: src/mcp/server.rs

The expression usize::try_from(u32_val.min(N)).unwrap_or(N) is functionally correct on 64-bit targets (u32 always fits in usize) but the try_from + unwrap_or pattern implies fallibility that doesn't exist on the target platform.

Two acceptable resolutions:
1. Use `u32_val.min(N) as usize` with a safety comment: `// safe: u32 always fits in usize on 64-bit targets`
2. Use `.expect("u32 always fits in usize on 64-bit platforms")` — this self-documents the assumption and panics only on exotic 16-bit targets (which we do not support)

Option 2 is preferred: it makes the assumption explicit as a contract assertion rather than silently falling back to N on hypothetical 16-bit platforms.

Add a compile-time static assertion if desired: `const _: () = assert!(std::mem::size_of::<usize>() >= 4);`
