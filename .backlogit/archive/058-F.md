Eliminate two sibling check-then-use TOCTOU symlink/reparse swap vulnerabilities on the read-only permission mutation paths in src/db/store.rs by binding every permission operation to an identity-bound, no-follow file handle instead of re-resolving paths. Covers BOTH EngineReadonlyGuard lock/acquisition-rollback/Drop restore (main db index 0 + all sidecars) and clear_stale_readonly_lock write-mode sidecar permission probe/clear. Fail-closed on any link/reparse/unobtainable handle; NO path-based chmod fallback. Preserves exact-permission restoration and sidecar-content invariants. Consumes stash E86A6E56 (clear_stale_readonly_lock, high) and 5905CDEE (EngineReadonlyGuard, medium).
