## Historical Process Gap — Evidence Recorded After Archival Relocation (recorded 2026-08-30, PR #114 review comment 3890182212)

The revised reconciliation skill and Ship authorization require commit evidence
(`track_commit`-equivalent) to be recorded **before** an artifact is archived/relocated
out of `.backlogit/queue/`. This run's actual sequence recorded commit evidence (step 2
above) only *after* step 1 had already routed `051-S` into `.backlogit/archive/` — step 1's
relocation was itself the archival event, and step 2 ran against the already-archived
artifact. Step 2 preceding step 3's terminal archive-marker call does not satisfy the
evidence-before-archival contract, because the contract is violated at the point of
relocation (step 1), not at the point of the final marker call (step 3). This historical
ordering gap is distinct from the evidence-value remediation described in the note below:
that note corrects *which* SHA was recorded; this note corrects the record to reflect
*when*, relative to archival, the (eventually correct) SHA was recorded. Neither is
correctable retroactively — the actual 2026-08-29 execution order is permanent history.
`fcbd6e8` (already on this branch prior to this correction) subsequently split the
safe-close skill's atomic-archive and track-commit-then-archive paths into mutually
exclusive options specifically so a future run cannot reproduce this ordering gap.
